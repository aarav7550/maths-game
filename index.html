About us
Numbers
A small daily drill for mental math, built to make CAT prep less painful. Pick a skill, race the clock, and watch your speed and accuracy improve over time.

Changelog
v2.0.0Current
  • New skills: Square, Cube, Tables and Percentage Reciprocals
  • Keyboard shortcuts: press a skill's first letter to open it, Shift + H for History (then a letter for that skill's history), Shift + M for the menu, 1–5 to pick a level, Enter to start, Esc to go back. See them all with Ctrl + . or under Keyboard shortcuts in the 3 dot menu
v1.0.0First release
  • Halving, ×2, ×3, and Additions drills, plus a Mixed mode
  • Speed and accuracy history with trend graphs

Keyboard shortcuts
Best on a laptop or desktop. Press Ctrl + . any time to open or close this.
Home
Letter
Press a skill's first letter to open its difficulty picker. If skills share a letter, press it again to move to the next one.
Shift+H
Open your History.
Shift+M
Open the 3 dot menu.
History
Letter
Open that skill's history. Same letters and cycling as on Home. On a skill's history page they switch to another skill.
Skill letters
Difficulty picker
Letter
Jump straight to another skill's picker. Same cycling as on Home.
1–5
Pick a level, in the order shown (Custom is last).
Enter
Start the round.
The 3 dot menu
↑↓
Move between the options. The first one is highlighted when the menu opens with Shift + M.
Enter
Open the highlighted option.
Popups and screens
Esc
Close a popup or the menu, or go back one screen. During a round it opens “Leave this round?” (press Esc again to stay).
←→↑↓
Move between a popup's buttons. Enter presses the highlighted one.
